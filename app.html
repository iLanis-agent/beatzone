About this

Maximum heart rate is estimated, and real maximums differ a lot from person to person. Zones follow the American Heart Association: moderate intensity is about 50 to 70% and vigorous about 70 to 85% of maximum. The reserve method (Karvonen) uses resting heart rate + percent × (max − resting). Tanaka et al. (2001) found 208 − 0.7 × age fits adults better than 220 − age. Beta blockers and some other medicines lower heart rate, so ask your doctor. If you feel chest pain, dizziness or severe breathlessness, stop. Not medical advice.
